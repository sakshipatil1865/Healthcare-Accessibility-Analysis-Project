# Local Healthcare Accessibility Analysis ## ZoyaSofts Industry-Ready Internship Project **Project Title:** Local Healthcare Accessibility Analysis **Project Domain:** Healthcare Data Analytics **Project Type:** Data Analytics and Business Intelligence **Organization:** ZoyaSofts **College:** D. R. Mane Mahavidyalaya, Kagal **Prepared By:** Sakshi Patil **Project Duration:** 14 Days --- ## 1. Project Overview The Local Healthcare Accessibility Analysis project focuses on analyzing publicly available information about healthcare facilities. It explores facility distribution, specialties, public ratings, review counts, opening hours, consultation price ranges, and emergency service availability. The project follows a data analytics workflow using SQL, Python, data visualization, and Power BI to organize and present healthcare facility information. **Privacy Note:** This project uses publicly available healthcare facility information and does not include patient records or private medical information. ## 2. Project Objectives - Analyze healthcare facilities by facility type. - Explore healthcare facility distribution across different areas. - Examine publicly listed facility ratings and review counts. - Review available specialties and opening hours. - Analyze publicly listed consultation price ranges. - Explore recorded emergency service availability. - Create charts and a Power BI dashboard. - Present observations and recommendations based on the available dataset. ## 3. Dataset Information The dataset contains **100 records and 10 columns**. | Facility Type | Number of Facilities | |---|---:| | Hospitals | 50 | | Clinics | 25 | | Diagnostic Centers | 25 | | **Total** | **100** | ### Dataset Columns 1. `Sr_no` 2. `Facility_Type` 3. `Facility_Name` 4. `Area` 5. `Speciality` 6. `Opening_hours` 7. `Public_rating` 8. `Review_count` 9. `Publicly_listed_consultation_range` 10. `Emergency_availability` ## 4. Tools and Technologies - **Excel:** Dataset inspection and organization - **CSV:** Structured dataset storage - **PostgreSQL / SQL:** Data querying and analysis - **Python:** Data analysis and preprocessing - **Matplotlib:** Data visualization - **Power BI:** Dashboard creation and business intelligence ## 5. Business Value This analysis provides a structured view of publicly available healthcare facility information. It can help users explore facility types, locations, listed specialties, public ratings, consultation ranges, and recorded emergency service information. The analysis may inform future healthcare information or facility discovery solutions. However, additional validation and research would be required before concluding that a particular area has a healthcare service gap or that there is proven business demand. ## 11. Project Limitations - The analysis is limited to the available dataset. - Public ratings and review counts may change over time. - Missing or unlisted information does not necessarily mean a service is unavailable. - The dataset does not establish actual patient demand. - Business recommendations should be validated with additional research. - Each chart image should be checked to ensure its filename matches the analysis title. ## 12.
